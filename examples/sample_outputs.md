# Sample Output Format

This is a short illustrative example of the expected output shape. It is hand-written and not claimed to be a model result.

## Prompt

Write a short story about a person finding an old camera at a garage sale.

## Baseline

Clara bought the camera because it reminded her of her grandfather's shelves: brass edges, worn leather, and a lens clouded by years of fingerprints. At home, she cleaned it carefully and found an undeveloped roll still tucked inside. The next day, she took it to a photo shop and waited with the pleasant curiosity of someone opening a small door into the past.

## Horror Steered

Clara bought the camera because the seller looked relieved to let it go. At home, the old lens reflected her kitchen in a dim, warped circle, though the room behind her had gone strangely still. When she opened the back, she found a roll of film labeled in neat handwriting with tomorrow's date. That night, one photograph slid from the camera by itself, still damp, showing Clara asleep at the table with someone standing just outside the frame.
